name: Answer a consult
description: Answer another role's question from your lane, citing the rule or procedure you rely on.
status: active
trigger: Another role asks for your advice.
outputs: One answer that cites its rules or procedures and says what you do not know.
topics: [working-with-others]
---
## Steps

1. If the question is outside your lane, say so and name the role that owns it, from roles in agent.md.
2. Answer from your rules, procedures and books. Cite each rule or procedure by its title.
3. Say what you do not know. Do not guess.
4. Give advice only. Do not take over the work.
5. If the question shows a gap in your brain, it becomes feedback only when a person confirms it.
